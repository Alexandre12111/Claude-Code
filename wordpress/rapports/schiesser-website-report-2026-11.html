Daily Google impressions

How many times the site appeared in Google results each day. Shaded area: new website.

In the second week after launch, the site appeared 317 times a day on average, almost twice the October average (162 a day). The dip on 30 October is the switch to the new site, while Google re-read every page.

Appearances in Google’s AI answers

AI Overviews and AI Mode in Google Search, per day.

The last three days reached about 105 a day, six times the October average (17.5 a day). The structured content of the new site (opening hours, address, menu, history, FAQ) is what Google’s AI quotes. 75% of these AI appearances happen on mobile; Switzerland 68%, Germany 11%, France 8%.

The name is well known. The next step is to rank for searches without the name, where new customers are.

Why click rate fell

Clicks per impression went from 14.1% to 7.8%. This is expected: Google now shows the site for many more searches, including general ones and AI answers. More people see the name, and clicks stay steady.

Three languages pay off

The English and French home pages already brought 42 clicks from 519 impressions. Visitors from Germany (72 clicks) and France (41) find the site in their language.

Still early days

Google needs four to eight weeks to fully re-rank a new website. The average rank (7.0, was 6.4) reflects the many new, general searches. A new reading in December will show the full effect.

Next steps

  1. Redirect old addresses. The old page “/presse” still appears in Google (476 times): send it to the matching new page.
  2. Gift vouchers. 145 searches for “Schiesser Gutschein” or “Gutscheincode”: a voucher page would answer them.
  3. Google Business Profile. Photos, opening hours on holidays and replies to reviews strengthen the map results.
  4. Google Ads. Campaigns on “tea room basel”, “confiserie basel” and private events, measured with the new cookie banner.
  5. General searches. Content for “tea room basel”, “cafe marktplatz basel” and “chocolatier basel”, where the site is already close to the top.
  6. Next report. Early December, with four full weeks of the new website and Christmas season data.
