In short

How do you redesign a website without losing your SEO?

A website redesign means rebuilding an existing website, often one that has become slow, dated or ineffective, without losing the visibility it has earned on Google. The main risk of a redesign is losing traffic: pages disappear or change address, and the rankings you had collapse. To avoid this, Aurea Media starts with an audit of the pages that receive visits, the keywords you rank for and your inbound links. Every old address is then redirected, with a permanent 301 redirect, to the matching new page, never to the home page by default. Useful content is kept, and content that doesn't convert is rewritten. After launch, Search Console is monitored for thirty days to fix any errors. A business website redesign starts at €1,000 excl. VAT, and the initial audit is free before the quote.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about redesigns.

Will I lose my Google rankings?

No, if the migration is prepared: an audit of the pages that rank, keeping useful content, 301 redirects page by page and monitoring in Search Console.

Does everything need rewriting?

Not necessarily. We keep the content that works, rewrite what doesn't convert and create the missing pages.

How much does a redesign cost?

The same as a new build, from €1,000 excl. VAT for a business website. The initial audit is free before the quote.

Does my website stay online during the work?

Yes. The new website is built separately and launched in one go, with no downtime.
