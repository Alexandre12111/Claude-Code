In short

What should a law firm website include?

A law firm website should let a client understand within seconds whether the firm handles their problem, what it might cost and how to book an appointment. It usually includes one page per practice area, a presentation of the lawyers and their background, a page dedicated to fees, practical information and a way to book an appointment, at the office or by video call. In France, lawyers have been allowed to advertise personally since 2014, provided they respect the core principles of the profession: dignity, fairness and confidentiality. Mandatory information, such as the bar the lawyer is registered with, must appear on the website. Frequently asked questions

Questions from lawyers.

Can a lawyer advertise on their website?

Yes. In France, personal advertising has been allowed since 2014, provided the core principles of the profession are respected: dignity, fairness, confidentiality. The website is written accordingly.

Can fees be displayed?

Yes, and it's recommended: a clear fees page (fixed fees, hourly rate, fee agreement) reassures clients and qualifies enquiries.
