How much does a website cost at Aurea Media?

At Aurea Media, a custom business website starts at €1,000 excl. VAT and an e-commerce website at €1,650 excl. VAT. Brand identity, motion design and presentations are priced on quote, depending on the scope. The final price of a website depends on three things: the number of pages, the features, such as online booking, multiple languages or a blog, and how much copywriting is entrusted to the agency. Every price includes bespoke design, mobile and tablet adaptation, technical SEO, structured data, the contact form, Search Console setup and training to manage the site. There is no mandatory subscription: only hosting and the domain name are paid each year, in the client's name, and the client remains the owner of their website. The line-by-line detailed quote is free and sent within 24 hours.

Updated on 25 October 2026 by Alexandre Coury, founder of Aurea Media.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about price.

Why “from”?

The final price depends on the number of pages, the features and the copywriting. The detailed quote fixes the price before the project starts: it doesn't change afterwards.

How does payment work?

The terms and payment schedule are set out in the quote, before any commitment.

Is there a subscription?

No. Only hosting and the domain name are annual, in your name. Maintenance is optional.

Is SEO extra?

Technical SEO is included. Writing regular articles is an option.
