Why does a tradesperson need a website?

Before calling a tradesperson, a client checks four things: work comparable to their project, the service area, signs of reliability and an easy way to request a quote. A website for a tradesperson or construction company answers these questions better than a simple directory listing. It shows jobs in before and after photos, added from a phone at the end of each job, the towns covered, insurance (such as the French ten-year building guarantee), qualifications such as RGE or Qualibat, and client reviews. A call button and a quote form stay visible on mobile. Combined with an optimised Google Business Profile, the website helps you get found locally. Aurea Media notably created the websites of Novexia Construction, Normande d'Isolation and TCM Grand Est. A tradesperson's website starts at €1,000 excl. VAT and goes live in about four weeks.
