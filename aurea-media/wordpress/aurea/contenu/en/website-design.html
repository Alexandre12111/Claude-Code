What is a custom business website?

A business website (also called a brochure or showcase website) presents a company, its services and its work, without selling online. Its job is to reassure prospects, be found on Google and trigger contact: a call, a quote request or a meeting. A custom business website is designed specifically for the business, instead of reusing a template shared by thousands of other sites. At Aurea Media, a business website usually has five to eight pages: home, services, portfolio, about and contact, plus pages per trade or per city when the SEO strategy justifies it. It starts at €1,000 excl. VAT, technical SEO included, and goes live in four weeks on average. The client owns the website and updates it themselves thanks to the training included.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a custom business website cost?

From €1,000 excl. VAT. The price depends on the number of pages, the features (booking, multilingual, blog) and the content to write. The detailed quote is free and sent within 24h.

How many pages does a business website need?

Usually 5 to 8 pages: home, services, portfolio, about, contact, and pages per service or per city if your SEO strategy justifies it.

Who writes the copy?

You can supply your own copy or have us write it. Key pages are written for Google and for your clients.

Are hosting and the domain name included?

We help you choose fast hosting and register the domain name, in your name. You keep ownership of everything.

What happens after launch?

You are trained to update your website. Monthly maintenance (updates, backups, security) is available as an option.
