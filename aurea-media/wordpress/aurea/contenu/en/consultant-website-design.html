In short

What should a coach or consultant website show?

A coach or consultant website has to build trust before the first conversation, because this kind of service is rarely chosen on price. It clearly sets out the positioning: for whom, on which topics and with which method. Offers are presented without jargon, with their formats and, where possible, a starting price or a price range. A discovery call is booked directly in the calendar, with a tool such as Calendly. An easy-to-update blog lets you publish articles on your areas of expertise, which are found on Google and can be cited by answer engines such as ChatGPT or Perplexity. References, publications and speaking engagements provide the proof. Aurea Media notably created the websites of Sultan Conseil & Leviers, Enzym and INAS. A consultant website starts at €1,000 excl. VAT and goes live in about four weeks.
