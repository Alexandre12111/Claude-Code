What is Aurea Media?

Aurea Media is an independent web design agency founded in 2021 by Alexandre Coury, a developer and designer based in Paris. The agency creates bespoke business websites and e-commerce stores for freelancers, small businesses and SMEs in France and across Europe. Each website is designed for its market, built on WordPress without a page builder and delivered with complete technical SEO: tags, structured data, loading speed and Search Console. A business website starts at €1,000 excl. VAT and an e-commerce website at €1,650 excl. VAT, with a detailed quote sent within 24 hours. The average time to launch is four weeks. More than 45 websites have been delivered, with a rating of {{avis_note}} out of 5 from {{avis_total}} Google reviews. The agency also designs brand identities, motion design videos and sales presentations.

Frequently asked questions

Before you get started.

How much does a website cost?

A bespoke business website starts at €1,000 excl. VAT and an e-commerce website at €1,650 excl. VAT. The final price depends on the number of pages, the features and the content to produce. You receive a free, detailed quote within 24h.

How long does it take to build a website?

Allow 4 weeks for a business website and 6 to 8 weeks for an e-commerce website, depending on the size of the catalogue.

Is Google SEO included?

Yes, at no extra cost: tag structure, structured data, speed, internal linking, sitemap and Search Console setup.

Will I be able to update my website myself?

Yes. Your text, photos, projects and articles are edited from a simple interface, and training is included at handover.

Do you work outside Paris?

Yes. We work with clients all over France and Europe (Switzerland, Belgium, Portugal). Everything is done remotely, with regular video calls.
