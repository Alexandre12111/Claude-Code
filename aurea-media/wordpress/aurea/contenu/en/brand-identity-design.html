What does a brand identity include?

A brand identity is the set of graphic elements that make a business recognisable: the logo, the colour palette, the typefaces and the rules governing their use. It differs from the logo alone, which is only one part of it. At Aurea Media, a brand identity includes the logo in all its variations (primary version, monogram, light and dark versions and favicon), the palette with its HEX, RGB and CMYK codes, a pair of typefaces suited to web and print, and PDF brand guidelines explaining margins, minimum sizes and forbidden uses. The work starts with a questionnaire about your values and clientele, continues with three creative directions and ends with refining the chosen direction. All source files, vector and editable, are transferred to the client at delivery.
