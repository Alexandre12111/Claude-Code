What should a healthcare professional's website include?

A healthcare professional's website should let a patient know within seconds whether the practitioner treats their problem, where the practice is and how to book an appointment. It presents the practitioners, their specialities and the languages they speak, the treatments offered, opening hours, access and accessibility, with a link to the booking tool, such as Doctolib, on every page. The tone stays informative: in France, since 2020, the medical code of ethics has allowed doctors to communicate about their practice, provided the information is fair, honest and non-commercial. Other healthcare professions follow similar rules set by their professional bodies. Aurea Media notably created the website of the Xpert Medical network of medical centres. A practice website starts at €1,000 excl. VAT, including writing the speciality pages, and goes live in about four weeks.

Frequently asked questions

Questions from practitioners.

Can a doctor have a website?

Yes. In France, since 2020, the medical code of ethics has allowed doctors to share information about their practice, provided it is fair, honest and non-commercial. The website is written accordingly.

Can Doctolib be integrated into the website?

Yes, with a booking button on every page, or an embedded widget if your tool allows it.
