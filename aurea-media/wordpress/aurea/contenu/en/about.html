In short

Who is behind Aurea Media?

Aurea Media is an independent web design agency founded in 2021 by Alexandre Coury, a developer and designer, and based on rue François Ier in Paris. The studio designs and builds custom business websites and online stores on WordPress and WooCommerce, as well as brand identities, motion design videos and sales presentations. Every project is run by the same person, from the first meeting to launch, with no sales team and no subcontracting. More than 45 websites have been delivered since 2021, for freelancers, small and medium businesses and institutions, in Paris, the Île de France region and across Europe, including Geneva, Lisbon and Brussels. Every site comes with careful technical SEO, structured data and training, and clients keep full ownership of their website, content and domain name, with no mandatory subscription.
