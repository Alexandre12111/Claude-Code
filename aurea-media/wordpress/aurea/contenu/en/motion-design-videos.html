What is motion design?

Motion design is the animation of shapes, text, logos and illustrations to create a video, without filming with a camera. It is used to explain an offer in a few seconds, bring a brand to life or capture attention on social media. The most requested formats are logo animations of three to six seconds, explainer videos of sixty to ninety seconds with voice-over and subtitles, and short vertical formats for LinkedIn, Instagram or TikTok. The brand film shown on this page was designed and animated by Aurea Media for its own communication. Every project follows four stages approved with the client: the script, the storyboard, the animation with music, then delivery in every useful format. On a website, the video is compressed and only loads when it becomes visible, so it doesn't slow the pages down.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about motion design.

How much does a motion design video cost?

On quote depending on length and complexity: a logo animation costs far less than a 90-second explainer video with voice-over.

How long does it take?

One to two weeks for a logo animation, three to four for an explainer video.

Will the video slow down my website?

No: it is compressed, only loaded when it becomes visible and paused off-screen, just like on this website.

Can I use it everywhere?

Yes, the usage rights are transferred to you for all your media.
