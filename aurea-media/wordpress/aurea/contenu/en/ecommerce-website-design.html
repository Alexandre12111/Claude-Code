How much does a custom e-commerce website cost?

A custom e-commerce website by Aurea Media starts at €1,650 excl. VAT. It is built with WooCommerce, the online selling plugin for WordPress, which avoids the monthly subscriptions and per-sale fees of closed platforms. The final price mainly depends on the number of products to add, the payment and shipping methods, and features such as subscriptions, multiple languages or trade pricing. Payments go through Stripe or PayPal, with Apple Pay and Google Pay, and the money goes straight into the merchant's account. Every store is delivered with product pages optimised for Google, a short checkout and an admin you can use from your phone. Delivery takes six to eight weeks depending on the size of the catalogue, and existing products can be imported from Shopify, Wix or PrestaShop.

Updated on 25 October 2026 by Alexandre Coury, founder of Aurea Media.

Frequently asked questions

Everything about your online store.

How much does an e-commerce website cost?

From €1,650 excl. VAT. The price depends on the number of products, the payment and shipping methods and the features (subscriptions, B2B, multilingual).

Can you take over my existing store?

Yes. We migrate products, customers and orders from Shopify, Wix or PrestaShop, with redirects to keep your rankings.

Which shipping methods?

Colissimo, Mondial Relay, Chronopost, in-store pickup or local delivery, with automatic shipping cost calculation.

Will the website be fast?

Yes: custom theme, optimised images and caching. A fast store sells more, especially on mobile.
