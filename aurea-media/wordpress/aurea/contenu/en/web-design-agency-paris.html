Why choose an independent web design agency in Paris?

An independent web design agency in Paris gives you a single point of contact, from the first meeting to launch, whereas a large agency splits a project between a salesperson, a project manager and subcontractors. Aurea Media is a Paris studio founded in 2021 by Alexandre Coury, who designs and builds every website himself. Meetings take place in Paris, at the client's premises in Île-de-France or by video call. For local businesses, the website comes with local SEO work: Google Business Profile optimisation, pages per town or neighbourhood where relevant, and structured data describing the business. Prices are the same everywhere, from €1,000 excl. VAT for a business website and €1,650 excl. VAT for an e-commerce website, with a detailed quote within 24 hours and launch in four weeks on average.
