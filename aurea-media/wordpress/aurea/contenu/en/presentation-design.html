What makes a good sales presentation?

A good sales presentation tells a simple story: the client's problem, the proposed solution, the proof that it works and the action expected at the end. Each slide carries a single idea, readable in a few seconds, with little text, highlighted figures and visuals consistent with the company's identity. Aurea Media designs pitch decks, sales proposals and company presentations in PowerPoint, Keynote or Google Slides. The presentation shown on this page is the one the agency sends to its own prospects: five slides for the promise, the method, the proof, the portfolio and the investment. The deck is delivered with reusable slide templates, so the client can create their next presentations on their own. A presentation of ten to twenty slides takes about a week of work, depending on the content provided and how much copywriting is needed.
