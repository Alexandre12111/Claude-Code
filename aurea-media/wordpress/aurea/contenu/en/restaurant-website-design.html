In short

What should a restaurant website offer?

A restaurant website should answer a guest's four questions within seconds: what's on the menu, when it's open, where the venue is and how to book. The menu, opening hours, address and a booking button are therefore visible as soon as the page opens, especially on phones, where most visits come from. The menu is edited from the admin, without needing a provider, and the booking tool you already use, such as TheFork or Zenchef, is integrated into the website. Google reviews can be displayed and updated automatically. A page dedicated to groups, private hire and events, with its own form, completes the picture. Aurea Media notably created the websites of Villa Moniris, Cercle La Raison and Domaine Duchentyls. A restaurant website starts at €1,000 excl. VAT and goes live in about four weeks.
